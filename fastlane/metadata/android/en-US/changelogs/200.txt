Jellydroid 2.0, based on Findroid 1.1.0.

Highlights:
- Responsive home and library layouts for phones, tablets and foldables
- Browse by genre, tag, rating, year, actor, director, writer and studio
- Random library sort, poster/still cover toggle, cached search results
- Editable poster and NFO metadata with multi-site scraping
- Optional AI translation of scraped titles and overviews, off by default
- Downloads continue in background, with concurrent segments and resume
- Pick between multiple versions and capture the current frame as a backdrop
- Support for Jellyfin 10.10.x servers

Plus a bunch of UI fixes and stability improvements.
